**How to read this**

- **Views/sub** — median views divided by the publishing channel's subscriber count.
  Above ~1× means the algorithm is pushing videos well past their existing audience,
  so a channel with no subscribers can still get seen. Below ~0.3× means reach is
  mostly going to channels that already have an audience.
- **New entrants <12mo** — share of the sampled channels created in the last year.
  A high number is the strongest available evidence that the niche is still open.
- **MFK share** — share flagged `madeForKids`. High means the niche lives under the
  COPPA ad regime (~$1–3 RPM, no comments/memberships). Low means competitors are
  reaching this audience through general-audience content and keeping full monetization.
- **Openness** — heuristic blend of the two signals above. Sorting aid, not a verdict.

> Sampling caveat: `order=viewCount` returns the head of the distribution, so
> "median views" here is the median *of the winners*, not of the niche. It measures
> the ceiling. Re-run with `--order relevance` to sample the typical case instead.

> **Sample overlap** — these niches drew some of the same videos, so their rows are
> not independent observations. Tighten the queries in the config to separate them:
>
> - Character-led episodic mini-stories ∩ Generic AI story cartoons (enforcement risk): **8 shared videos** (10% of the smaller sample)
> - Character-led episodic mini-stories ∩ Nursery rhymes / songs (incumbent wall): **5 shared videos** (9% of the smaller sample)
> - Character-led episodic mini-stories ∩ Preschool edutainment (colors/numbers/phonics): **1 shared video** (1% of the smaller sample)
